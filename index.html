01 — About

Who’s behind the segfaults

I didn’t start out wanting to be a developer. In first year, a C programming lab threw me in at the deep end — segfaults, pointers, and the quiet thrill of the first program I actually understood. Ever since, I’ve been building the kind of software professors assign and people actually use: a library system, a CGPA calculator that made my attendance spreadsheet obsolete, too many terminal games.

Right now I’m in my third year of BCA, working through data structures, DBMS and operating systems in class while teaching myself the bits the syllabus skips — clean UI, version control habits, and how to ship something nobody needs a manual to use.

I like the middle ground: small teams, real problems, and code that has a job to do.
